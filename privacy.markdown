# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

<div class="separator"></div>

Welcome to the **Mind Matters** conclave website, managed by the **Peer Educators Initiative** at **Delhi Public School Bangalore South**. We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our participants, delegates, teachers, and visitors.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you register for Mind Matters conclave events, submit delegation applications, or reach out to our team, we may collect the following information:
* **Contact Information:** Full name, institutional email address, contact phone number, and school/college affiliation.
* **Participant Details:** Class/Grade, delegation size, event category selections, and faculty guide details.
* **Media & Submissions:** Competition entries such as research abstracts, presentation decks, videos, or reels submitted for evaluation.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
The collected information is used solely for legitimate purposes related to the event, including:
* Processing and validating event registrations and delegations.
* Sending official communications, event schedules, participation badges, and certificates.
* Verifying eligibility criteria and coordinating logistics with participating schools.
* Assessing and judging competitive events.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Security
* We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
* Information collected through third-party platforms (such as Entrar or Google Forms) is processed in accordance with their respective security and privacy standards.
* Internal records are stored securely and accessible only to authorized event coordinators and school counsellors.

---

### 4. Photography & Media
Mind Matters is a live regional conclave. Photographs, audio, and video recordings may be taken during presentations, competitions, and ceremonies for school archival, educational, and awareness purposes.
