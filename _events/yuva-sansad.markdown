## Rules and Regulations
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students of classes IX, X, XI, and XII can register.
- **Team Size:** Maximum 5 participants per school / college / delegation.
- **Topic:** "The Mental Health Bill, 2026".
- Participants must research topics to find policies and their implementations in accordance with the ten ministries.
- Participants must carry their research material with them.
- Laptops and mobiles are to be carried, provided they are used effectively.
- Stakeholders will be assigned to the students through chits on Day 2.
- Participants will be given 45 minutes for preparation.
- Each ministry gets to speak for 3 minutes.
- Opposition ministries must note down points and question the policies and implementations of other ministries.
- **Decorum & Conduct:** Participants must maintain a respectful, parliamentary tone throughout the debate. Active listening and constructive policy critique are mandatory.
