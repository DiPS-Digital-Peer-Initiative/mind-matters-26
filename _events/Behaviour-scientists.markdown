**Behaviour Scientists** is a platform for young minds to step into the fascinating world of psychological and behavioural research. It encourages students to move beyond textbooks, ask meaningful questions, explore real-world behaviour, and present their findings using the rigorous principles of scientific research.

## Categories

### Category A: Young Researcher | Grades XI & XII
Students, under the guidance and supervision of a teacher/mentor, undertake a small-scale research study based on psychological themes. Participants develop their research hypothesis, collect and analyse primary or secondary data, and present their work in the form of a research paper. The process includes abstract submission followed by full paper submission, adhering strictly to APA guidelines.

### Category B: Research Explorer | Grades IX & X
Young researchers explore a key theme or empirical question related to human behaviour and present their investigation through an engaging, visual research poster presentation. This category offers early experience with the scientific inquiry process and visual communication of behavioral insights.

## Rules and Guidelines
- **Eligibility:** Students from Grades IX, X, XI, and XII.
- **Team Size:** Team of 2 students per school / college / delegation.
- **Academic Standards:** Follow standard APA (American Psychological Association) guidelines for both Paper Presentation and Poster formats.
- **Title Consistency:** Ensure the final title of your Paper or Poster matches exactly with the title submitted in your abstract.
- **Reference Samples:** Refer to the official Mind Matters Instagram page ([@dips_mindmatters26](https://instagram.com/dips_mindmatters26)) for samples, formatting rubrics, and further details.
