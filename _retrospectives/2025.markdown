# Mind Matters 2025

The Young Adolescent Peer Educator’s Leadership Conclave – Mind Matters 2025 continued its legacy of promoting mental health awareness and education among young minds under the guidance of the Master Peer Educators and Peer Educators of Delhi Public School, Bangalore South. The event was organised on 10th and 11th October, 2025 to celebrate World Mental Health Day and encourage students to engage in meaningful conversations about mental well-being. The conclave witnessed active participation from students across various schools and delegates, providing them with a platform to exchange ideas, develop empathy and spread awareness about mental health. Inspired by the success of the previous editions, Mind Matters 2025 continued to emphasise the importance of creating supportive environments for young people and breaking the stigma surrounding mental health.

The two-day event featured a range of competitions, including Nukkad Natak, Yuva Sansad, Natyalaya, JAM, Pitch Perfect, Laugh it Out, Utopia and Reel Making. These competitions encouraged students to think critically about mental wellness and express their perspectives through creative and innovative approaches. Beyond the diverse array of competitions, Mind Matters 2025 featured an engaging panel discussion, interactive stalls and insightful workshops designed for parents and teachers, led by accomplished professionals and experts in their respective fields.

In its entirety, Mind Matters 2025 proved to be a successful conglomerate of initiatives, meticulously organised by the Peer Educators of Delhi Public School, Bangalore South.
