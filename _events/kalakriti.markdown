**KALAKRITI** is a poster-making competition that invites participants to creatively explore and promote critical themes surrounding mental health. Through visual expression, students will raise awareness, challenge societal taboos, and share positive messages related to emotional well-being, resilience, communication, and inclusion. The event aims to encourage thoughtful reflection and advocacy through art.

## Categories
- **Category 1:** Grades VIII & IX
- **Category 2:** Grade X & above

## Rules and Regulations
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students of classes VIII, IX, X, XI, and XII can register.
- **Participation:** Solo and only 2 participants per school / college / delegation.
- **Paper Provided:** All participants must use the official A3 art sheet provided by the host school.
- **Medium & Materials:** Posters may be made using pencils, colour pencils, crayons, sketch pens, markers, and oil pastels or paints. Photographs, pre-printed materials, wires, or 3D objects are strictly prohibited.
- **Originality:** The artwork must be 100% original, created on the spot by the participant. Any external assistance, pre-made designs, or plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.
- **Identification:** Participant details (Full Name, Class, School Name, and Contact Number) must be written exclusively on the back of the A3 sheet. No identifying markings should appear on the front.
