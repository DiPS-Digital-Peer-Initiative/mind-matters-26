Step into the fascinating world of the human mind! **The Mentalist** is an engaging, psychology-based experience that challenges participants to explore psychological concepts, human behaviour, mental health, emotions, personality, cognition, and perception.

Participants will encounter clues inspired by psychological theories, famous studies, mental health conditions, and their portrayal in films and popular culture. From understanding the mysteries of the mind to decoding behaviour, this experience will test how well you can observe, connect, analyse, and think like a psychologist. The mind holds the clues. Can you decode them?

## Rules and Regulations
- **Eligibility:** Students of Grades IX, X, XI, and XII.
- **Eligible Boards/Institutions:** Open to students from CBSE and other recognized boards.
- **Team Size:** 3 members per team. Cross-grade teams within the same institution are permitted.
- **Code of Conduct:**
  - Use of mobile phones or internet search during live rounds is strictly prohibited.
  - Device usage is allowed exclusively in Round 3 for slide formatting.
  - The judge's decision is final and binding.
