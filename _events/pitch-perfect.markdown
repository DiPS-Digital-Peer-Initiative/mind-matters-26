Unleash your entrepreneurial mind in **Pitch Perfect**, a platform to present creative and enterprising business ideas that promote mental health awareness, healing, and personal growth. Teams will create presentations proposing innovative models and solutions, pitching their ventures directly to our panel of judges.

## Rules and Regulations
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students of classes X, XI, and XII, along with 1st and 2nd year PU / college students, can register.
- **Team Composition:** Each team must consist of 1 to 3 students. Up to two teams per institution/delegation are permitted.
- **Presentation Format:** Every team must pitch their concept through a structured PowerPoint presentation detailing their business model, feasibility, and impact on mental well-being.
- **Pitch Duration:** Each team gets 10 to 12 minutes to pitch their idea to the judges, followed by a 5 to 7 minute Q&A round.
- **Originality:** Plagiarism is strictly prohibited. All business concepts and proposals must be authentic and original.
- **Evaluation:** Judges will assess feasibility, creativity, market need, social impact, and clarity of pitch. The judges’ decision will be final and binding.
