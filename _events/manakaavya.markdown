**Manakaavya** is a poetry writing competition that invites participants to transform thoughts into verse, exploring the many facets of the human mind and emotional landscape. Through topics centred on mental health, participants weave together raw emotions, personal reflections, and diverse perspectives to create evocative poetry that inspires understanding and awareness.

## Rules and Regulations
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students of classes X, XI, and XII can register.
- **Participation:** Solo entries only. Up to 2 individual participants are permitted from each school / college / delegation.
- **On-the-Spot Theme:** Participants must write an original poem based on the specific mental health topic allotted on the day of the competition.
- **Originality:** All poems must be composed on the spot during the competition. Any use of pre-written material, external reference, or AI assistance will lead to immediate disqualification.
- **Language & Form:** Poems may follow structured or free-verse styles, judged on emotional resonance, imagery, thematic depth, and poetic craftsmanship.
