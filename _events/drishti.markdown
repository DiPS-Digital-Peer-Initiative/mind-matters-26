**Drishti** is a photography event that encourages participants to visually depict issues surrounding mental health. Incorporating complex emotions and experiences, the event invites students to capture moments that are hard to put into words, using the camera lens to advocate for well-being and explore the challenges young people face.

## Topics

### Round 1
- **The Weight of the Backpack:** Academic pressure, expectations, and burnout.
- **The Everyday Mask:** Smiling while dealing with depression and the pressure to present a cheerful exterior.

### Round 2
- Assigned on the spot on the day of the event.

## Rules and Regulations
- **Eligibility:** Students of classes VIII, IX, X, XI, and XII can register.
- **Team Size:** Solo or up to 2 participants per school / college / delegation.
- **Theme Adherence:** The selected topic must be clearly depicted and interpreted in the submitted photographs.
- **Allowed Equipment:** One camera per team, props of the students' choosing, and a maximum of one model to depict the chosen topic.
- **Originality & Authenticity:** Submitted work must be 100% original, captured during the event by the participants. The use of generative AI tools, stock imagery, or plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.
- **Submission:** Photos must be submitted via the official Google Form provided on the day of the event, including school/delegation name, class, email address, and phone number. Late submissions will not be accepted.
