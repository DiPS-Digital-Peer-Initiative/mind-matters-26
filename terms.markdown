# Terms & Conditions

*Last updated: October 2026*

<div class="separator"></div>

These Terms & Conditions govern your participation in the **Mind Matters** conclave, organized by the **Peer Educators Initiative** at **Delhi Public School Bangalore South**. By registering or participating in any event, you agree to comply with the terms outlined below.

---

### 1. Eligibility & Registration
* **Eligibility:** Participation is open to students from classes VIII, IX, X, XI, and XII, as well as 1st and 2nd-year college students, subject to specific event guidelines.
* **School Delegations:** External school delegations must be accompanied by designated Teacher Coordinators and/or School Counsellors.
* **Accurate Information:** All information submitted during registration must be truthful and accurate. Providing false credentials may result in disqualification.

---

### 2. Code of Conduct & Decorum
* **Respectful Environment:** Mind Matters is an initiative promoting mental health awareness, empathy, and constructive dialogue. All participants, delegates, and guests must maintain a respectful, inclusive, and positive attitude.
* **Prohibited Behavior:** Harassment, discrimination, abusive language, or any form of disruptive conduct will result in immediate disqualification and removal from the premises.
* **Identification:** All participants must carry and display their valid school/college identity cards throughout the event days.

---

### 3. Competitions & Submissions
* **Originality:** All competitive submissions (reels, research papers, speeches, posters, performances) must be the original work of the participants. Plagiarism or copyright infringement will lead to disqualification.
* **Judges' Decisions:** Decisions made by judges and the conclave organizing committee are final and binding in all competitive events.
* **Time Limits:** Participants must strictly adhere to the time limits specified in individual event guidelines.
